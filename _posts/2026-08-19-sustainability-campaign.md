  <!-- How it works -->
  <section class="mb-5">
    <h2>How the campaign works?</h2>

    <p>
      A knowledge-drop campaign consists of a series of short communications published regularly in the weeks leading up to the conference. Each knowledge drop focuses on one sustainability topic and gives participants a concrete idea, fact, or action they can take.
    </p>

    
  </section>

  <section class="mb-5">
    <h2>What do I need to do?</h2>

    <p>
      1- <b>To set up your own campaign, you can start by defining a timeline</b>: your campaign can run weekly, bi-weekly, or according to the rhythm of your conference communication plan. 
    </p>

    
    <p>
      2- <b>Create the materials for the campaign</b>: download the materials available on this resources page and update the visuals to your campaign. 
    </p>

    <div class="table-responsive">
      <table class="table campaign-table align-middle">

    <p>
      3- <b> Set up the communication channels </b>: check which communication channels you will use to disseminate the campaign (LinkedIn, Websites, etc.). Schedule the posts with pictures and legends containing the appropriate information.
    </p>

    <p>
      4- <b>Check the engagement</b>: After releasing the posts, check the engagement with the publications and answer the comments (if applicable). Incentivate your conference committee to share the publications to increase visibility.
    </p>

  </section>
