Goal

The commitment with sustainability in conferences in software engineering is essential to reduce the environmental impact of the event. However, to organize an in-person scientific conference is not easy, knowledge sharing and fostering collaboration becomes essential to make those events indeed sustainable. There is many oportunities to reduce the ecologic footprint, particularly in terms of long-distance travel, resource consumption to organize and host the event, and waste generation during the conference.

Our vision in SUSConf is to deliver a framework that support organizers of conference that not only advances scientific research, but also maintains environmental responsibility. Therefore, SUSConf provides actionable strategies to minimize its environmental footprint and raise awareness of sustainability practices within the research community.

Who is with us?

SUSConf started at ECSA 2026 through a collaboration between Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam and the University of Bolzano. We are open-source; therefore, we invite the community to contribute ideas, suggestions, and improvements to the framework.
