# SUSTConf Metrics 

> (Work in Progress 🚧)


The **SUSTConf Metrics** project is an evolving evidence base for understanding how sustainability practices are being implemented in academic conferences. Our goal is for conferences adopting SUSTConf to share their measurements, helping us build evidence of which sustainability actions are effective and identify areas that deserve greater attention from the community.
