      <p class="lead">

        sustainability information at once, share small, useful pieces of
        knowledge with your community over time.

        <strong>Sustainability Knowledge Drops</strong> campaign for your
        conference. Adapt the topics, timing, examples and communication
        channels to your own event.

  <!-- How it works -->
  <section class="mb-5">
    <h2>How the campaign works</h2>

    <p>
      A knowledge-drop campaign consists of a series of short communications
      published regularly in the weeks leading up to the conference. Each
      knowledge drop focuses on one sustainability topic and gives participants
      a concrete idea, fact, or action they can take.
    </p>

    <div class="row g-4 mt-2">

      <div class="col-12 col-md-4">
        <div class="card h-100 shadow-sm border-0">
          <div class="card-body p-4">
            <div class="campaign-number">1</div>
            <h3 class="card-title">Inform</h3>
            <p class="card-text">
              Share a surprising fact, useful insight, or piece of evidence
              that helps participants understand why sustainability matters.
            </p>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>

      <div class="col-12 col-md-4">
        <div class="card h-100 shadow-sm border-0">
          <div class="card-body p-4">
            <div class="campaign-number">2</div>
            <h3 class="card-title">Inspire</h3>
            <p class="card-text">
              Show that sustainable choices can also be convenient, enjoyable,
              affordable and socially valuable.
            </p>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>

      <div class="col-12 col-md-4">
        <div class="card h-100 shadow-sm border-0">
          <div class="card-body p-4">
            <div class="campaign-number">3</div>
            <h3 class="card-title">Enable action</h3>
            <p class="card-text">
              Give participants practical suggestions, tools, links or
              alternatives that make sustainable behaviour easier.
            </p>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>

    </div>
  </section>

      post, while also providing enough information to be useful on the

  <h2>Suggested knowledge drops</h2>

  <div class="row g-4">

    <!-- Travel -->
    <div class="col-12">
      <div class="card shadow-sm border-0 campaign-topic-card">
        <div class="card-body p-4">

          <div class="topic-icon">🚆</div>

          <h3>01 · Sustainable travel</h3>

          <p class="topic-question">
            <strong>Did you know that …?</strong>
          </p>

          <p>
            Ground transportation can often be a lower-emission alternative
            to flying, particularly for journeys within the same country or
            continent.
          </p>

          <h4>Possible actions</h4>

          <ul>
            <li>Encourage participants to consider trains and buses.</li>
            <li>Provide practical travel information during registration.</li>
            <li>Share train routes, night-train options and journey planners.</li>
            <li>Make low-carbon travel visible in the conference programme.</li>
          </ul>

          <p class="mb-0">
            <strong>Useful resources:</strong>
            <a href="https://back-on-track.eu/night-train-map" target="_blank" rel="noopener">

              Trainline
            </a>,
            <a href="https://global.flixbus.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener">
              FlixBus
            </a>,
            <a href="https://www.rome2rio.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener">
              Rome2Rio
            </a>
          </p>

        </div>

    </div>



    <!-- Food -->
    <div class="col-12 mt-5">
      <div class="card shadow-sm border-0 campaign-topic-card">
        <div class="card-body p-4">

          <div class="topic-icon">🥗</div>

          <h3>02 · Sustainable food</h3>

          <p class="topic-question">
            <strong>Did you know that …?</strong>
          </p>

          <p>
            Eating less meat and dairy, particularly beef and lamb, can be
            one of the most effective ways individuals can reduce the carbon
            footprint associated with their food.
          </p>

          <h4>Possible actions</h4>

          <ul>
            <li>Offer attractive vegetarian and vegan options.</li>
            <li>Make plant-based meals the easy default.</li>
            <li>Highlight local and seasonal ingredients where appropriate.</li>
            <li>Share recommendations for sustainable food nearby.</li>
          </ul>

          <p class="mb-0">
            <strong>Useful resource:</strong>
            <a href="https://ourworldindata.org/food-choice-vs-eating-local"
               target="_blank" rel="noopener">
              Our World in Data – Food choices and environmental impact
            </a>
          </p>

        </div>
      </div>
    </div>


    <!-- Networking -->
    <div class="col-12 mt-5">
      <div class="card shadow-sm border-0 campaign-topic-card">
        <div class="card-body p-4">

          <div class="topic-icon">🤝</div>

          <h3>03 · Make the trip count</h3>

          <p class="topic-question">
            <strong>Did you know that …?</strong>
          </p>

          <p>
            In-person conferences can create opportunities for informal
            exchanges, spontaneous discussions, networking and collaboration.
            If participants travel to attend, encourage them to make the most
            of that journey.
          </p>

          <h4>Possible actions</h4>

          <ul>
            <li>Read the programme before travelling.</li>
            <li>Contact people you would like to meet in advance.</li>
            <li>Schedule several conversations during the same trip.</li>
            <li>Use social events and breaks for informal networking.</li>
            <li>Encourage collaboration that can reduce future travel.</li>
          </ul>

        </div>
      </div>
    </div>


    <!-- Carbon -->
    <div class="col-12 mt-5">
      <div class="card shadow-sm border-0 campaign-topic-card">
        <div class="card-body p-4">

          <div class="topic-icon">🌍</div>

          <h3>04 · Avoid, reduce, offset</h3>

          <p class="topic-question">
            <strong>Did you know that …?</strong>
          </p>

          <p>
            Carbon offsetting should not be considered a substitute for
            reducing emissions. A useful hierarchy is:
            <strong>avoid → reduce → offset</strong>.
          </p>

          <h4>Possible actions</h4>

          <ul>
            <li>Identify the largest sources of conference emissions.</li>
            <li>Avoid emissions where possible.</li>
            <li>Reduce unavoidable emissions through better choices.</li>
            <li>Only consider credible offsetting as a last resort.</li>
            <li>Be transparent about what the conference does and does not offset.</li>
          </ul>

          <p class="mb-0">
            <strong>Useful resource:</strong>
            <a href="https://www.computer.org/conferences/organize-a-conference#make-your-conference-more-environmentally-sustainable"
               target="_blank" rel="noopener">
              IEEE – Making conferences more environmentally sustainable
            </a>
          </p>

        </div>
      </div>
    </div>


    <!-- Digital -->
    <div class="col-12 mt-5">
      <div class="card shadow-sm border-0 campaign-topic-card">
        <div class="card-body p-4">

          <div class="topic-icon">💻</div>

          <h3>05 · Sustainable digital infrastructure</h3>

          <p class="topic-question">
            <strong>Did you know that …?</strong>
          </p>

          <p>
            Websites and digital services consume energy too. Sustainable
            digital design can reduce unnecessary data transfer and help
            minimise the environmental impact of online services.
          </p>

          <h4>Possible actions</h4>

          <ul>
            <li>Keep conference websites lightweight and efficient.</li>
            <li>Optimise images and other media.</li>
            <li>Avoid unnecessary animations and large downloads.</li>
            <li>Choose hosting providers powered by renewable energy.</li>
            <li>Check the environmental profile of your website.</li>
          </ul>

          <p class="mb-0">
            <strong>Useful resource:</strong>
            <a href="https://www.thegreenwebfoundation.org"
               target="_blank" rel="noopener">
              Green Web Foundation
            </a>
          </p>

        </div>
      </div>
    </div>


    <!-- Community -->
    <div class="col-12 mt-5">
      <div class="card shadow-sm border-0 campaign-topic-card">
        <div class="card-body p-4">

          <div class="topic-icon">💡</div>

          <h3>06 · Ask the community</h3>

          <p class="topic-question">
            <strong>Did you know that …?</strong>
          </p>

          <p>
            Your community may already have excellent ideas for making
            conferences more sustainable. Ask participants, programme
            committees and organisers what changes they would prioritise.
          </p>

          <h4>Possible actions</h4>

          <ul>
            <li>Run a short sustainability survey.</li>
            <li>Ask what changes participants value most.</li>
            <li>Publish the results openly.</li>
            <li>Turn recurring suggestions into concrete actions.</li>
            <li>Share successful ideas with future conferences.</li>
          </ul>

  <!-- Visual guidelines -->
  <section class="mb-5">
    <h2>Guidelines for campaign visuals</h2>

    <p>
      The visual identity should make the knowledge drops recognisable as a
      series while allowing each topic to have its own character.
    </p>

    <div class="card visual-guidelines-card border-0 shadow-sm">
      <div class="card-body p-4 p-lg-5">

        <div class="row g-4">

          <div class="col-12 col-md-6">
            <h3>Keep it recognisable</h3>
            <ul>
              <li>Use a consistent campaign title or hashtag.</li>
              <li>Keep typography and layout consistent.</li>
              <li>Give every knowledge drop a clear number.</li>
              <li>Use a recurring visual element to connect the series.</li>
            </ul>
          </div>

          <div class="col-12 col-md-6">
            <h3>Keep it simple</h3>
            <ul>
              <li>One main message per visual.</li>
              <li>Use short headlines rather than paragraphs.</li>
              <li>Make the action or recommendation obvious.</li>
              <li>Ensure visuals remain readable on mobile devices.</li>
            </ul>
          </div>

          <div class="col-12 col-md-6">
            <h3>Make it positive</h3>
            <ul>
              <li>Focus on opportunities rather than guilt.</li>
              <li>Show how sustainable choices can be enjoyable.</li>
              <li>Celebrate practical actions people can take.</li>
              <li>Avoid presenting sustainability as an impossible burden.</li>
            </ul>
          </div>

          <div class="col-12 col-md-6">
            <h3>Make it credible</h3>
            <ul>
              <li>Use reliable and preferably primary sources.</li>
              <li>Check statistics before publication.</li>
              <li>Link to further information where appropriate.</li>
              <li>Be transparent about uncertainty and limitations.</li>
            </ul>
          </div>

        </div>

      </div>
    </div>
  </section>

            <ul class="campaign-checklist">
              <li>Define the sustainability goals of the conference.</li>

              <li>Identify reliable sources for facts and statistics.</li>
              <li>Create a reusable visual template.</li>
            </ul>

          <div class="col-12 col-lg-6">

            <h3>For every knowledge drop</h3>

            <ul class="campaign-checklist">
              <li>Choose one clear message.</li>
              <li>Provide evidence or a reliable reference.</li>
              <li>Give participants a practical action.</li>
              <li>Adapt the message to the conference location.</li>
